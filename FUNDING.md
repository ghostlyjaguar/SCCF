# Support SCCF research

SCCF investigates how defensive systems distinguish strategic relationships from coincidence and prioritize further investigation under uncertainty. Financial support would fund reproducible experiments, independent validation, dataset preparation, maintenance and public research documentation.

The project does not manufacture threats, sell preferential benchmark scores, or guarantee security outcomes. Sponsors may suggest research questions, but cannot purchase a favorable finding or acceptance of a taxonomy class. Negative results remain publishable.

## Funding status
No sponsorship enrollment, payout account, recurring revenue, or paid service is verified. GitHub Sponsors enrollment and tax/payout setup must be completed by the account holder. See https://github.com/sponsors and https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors. The Sponsor button should be enabled only after the account holder confirms the destination. Financial support is voluntary and is not represented as tax-deductible. Licensing remains pending owner approval; public availability does not grant open-source reuse rights.

## Accountability
Track actual research expenses and verified funding separately; publish aggregate figures only with owner approval. Initial external API budget: zero. Propose any paid work with a spending cap, purpose and measurable acceptance criteria before authorization. Never automate payment, sponsor solicitation, or spending without explicit approval. No private banking details belong in this repository.
