# Draft governance

The repository owner retains final authority. Proposed classes remain drafts until documented review of the definition, evidence, alternatives, prior art and defensive relevance. Record decisions and disagreements publicly where appropriate. AI agents may propose changes but do not possess automatic approval authority. Changes to licensing, governance and accepted taxonomy require explicit maintainer approval. Independent review procedures and conflict-of-interest rules remain to be established.

## Foundation change control
[FOUNDATION.md](FOUNDATION.md) and [foundation/charter.json](foundation/charter.json) define the project scope. Any amendment requires a separate public proposal, explicit owner approval and a recorded rationale and version. Do not combine amendments with routine taxonomy or software changes. The owner may reject any change that conflicts with the charter.

## Acceptance gate
A candidate must have corroborated public evidence, prior-art comparison, exclusion criteria, a documented counterexample and independent review by someone other than the proposer. A review record must identify the reviewer and link to the public decision. Acceptance requires the owner's explicit decision; schema validity is never scientific validation. AI outputs alone are not independent human review. Use pull requests for changes; no auto-merging. Record classification revisions, preserve IDs and publish deprecations rather than deleting history.

## Repository enforcement
Require pull requests and passing validation on main; protect the charter, governance and validation controls from ordinary edits. CODEOWNERS documents review responsibility. Actual branch rules must be enabled by the repository owner; files alone do not enforce these settings. Until enforcement is confirmed, treat this policy as procedural.
